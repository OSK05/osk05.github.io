About

The DJ Behind the Decks

I'm DJ O·S·K, a UK based open format DJ performing at private events and club nights. I turn up prepared, read the room, and adapt to exactly what each event needs, from the first enquiry through to the final track.

With a wide range of experience across professional settings, I draw on a broad mix of styles and techniques to suit any crowd, on any night.

My focus is simple: do the job properly. That means performing well, reading the room, and delivering on what each client expects. My work over the past two years speaks for itself.

Equipment & Setup

Professional gear for every event

♬

Decks

I perform on the Pioneer XDJ-RX3, the same professional standard setup used in clubs across the UK. You get headline quality behind the decks, whether it is an intimate birthday or a packed venue.

🔊

Sound System

My rig scales to your event. I carry dual 15 inch main speakers and two 12 inch subwoofers for larger events, alongside two compact 8 inch speakers for smaller, more intimate settings. Clear, powerful sound at any size.

💡

Lighting

A full professional lighting rig comes as standard, including three par cans, two moving heads, a laser and a Chauvet multi effect unit. Your dancefloor will look and feel like a proper club night from the first track.

🆕

Professional Setup

I arrive with a professional DJ booth, speaker stands and lighting stands. Everything needed for a clean, polished setup that looks as good as it sounds. No makeshift rigs and no mess.

✅

Public Liability Insured

I am fully public liability insured, giving you complete peace of mind on the day. Most venues require this as standard, so I come fully covered and ready to go.

🏢

Any Event, Any Size

From an intimate gathering of thirty to a large wedding reception or club night, my setup handles it all. I assess every event individually and bring exactly what is needed, with no corners cut.
